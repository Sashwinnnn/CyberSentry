A Quick Story 📞

Stay Safe Online: A Friendly Guide to Protecting Yourself

Last year, a woman in a community just like ours got a call: "This is your bank. Your account is in danger." She did everything the voice on the phone told her to - and the caller was a criminal.

What would you have done? Today we learn how to spot these tricks, so they never work on you.

🔒 Passwords: Your Front Door Lock

❌ Weak Password

Short, one word, or a name.
Example: fluffy1

✅ Strong Passphrase

3-4 random words combined.
Example: BlueSkyHappyFish

Quick Tip: Use a different passphrase for your bank than you use for your everyday email.

🛡️ Two-Factor Authentication: The Second Lock

Even a great password can get stolen. Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second lock: after your password, the site asks for a short code from your phone.

Without 2FA:

A stolen password = a stolen account. The criminal walks right in.

With 2FA:

A stolen password is useless. The criminal doesn't have your phone, so the code stops them.

Quick Tip: Turn on 2FA for your bank and email first. A family member (or I) can help - it takes five minutes.

🎣 Phishing: Fake Bait

⚠️ Scam Example Email:

From: alerts@secure-bank-verify.com

"Your account is SUSPENDED! Click HERE now!"

  • Creates Panic: "Act NOW or lose everything!"
  • Strange Sender: Not from your real bank's true address.
  • Asks for Clicks: Real banks don't email you links to sign in.

📱 Phone & "Tech Support" Scams

⚠️ What it sounds like:

"This is Microsoft. Your computer has a virus. Give me access and I'll fix it for a small fee."

  • Real companies don't call you about viruses or refunds out of the blue.
  • Never give remote access to your computer to someone who called you.
  • Gift cards = scam. No real business or agency takes payment in gift cards.

👵 The "Grandchild in Trouble" Scam

A caller pretends to be your grandchild: "Grandma, I'm in trouble - I need money, and please don't tell Mom."

The Trick:

They use panic and secrecy so you send money before thinking.

The Defense:

Hang up and call your grandchild's real number, or ask a question only they would know.

🕵️ Spot the Scam: Real or Fake?

Let's practice! You make the call:

  • 1. An email says your bank account is suspended and gives you a link to "verify." (Fake - banks don't do this.)
  • 2. A "tech support" caller says your computer has a virus and asks for access. (Fake - hang up.)
  • 3. Your grandson texts from his usual number about Sunday dinner. (Real - but if he suddenly asks for money, call him first!)

🦠 Malware: Bad Software

Malware = "Malicious Software". Programs designed to spy or steal.

How it gets in:

• Suspicious email links
• "Free" unknown programs

How to stay safe:

• Keep computer updated
• Use antivirus software

⌨️ Keyloggers: A Quick Word

A keylogger is sneaky software that records what you type, including passwords. It hides in "free" downloads and suspicious email attachments.

The simple defense:

Don't download unknown programs, keep your computer updated, and use 2FA - a recorded password alone won't get a criminal in.

🆘 If You Think You've Been Scammed

It can happen to anyone - smart people, careful people. There is no shame in it. Act fast:

  • Call your bank right away using the number on the back of your card. They can stop payments and protect your account.
  • Tell a family member or friend. You don't have to handle it alone, and they can help with the next steps.
  • Report it to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov or 1-877-382-4357. Your report protects your neighbors too.

⭐ 3 Rules to Remember

1. Slow down

Scams run on panic. Real banks and agencies let you take your time.

2. Check first

Hang up and call back on a number you already trust before doing anything.

3. No gift cards, no remote access

Anyone who asks for either one is a criminal. Every time.

Imagine This 😱

Level Up: Essential Survival Guide for the Internet

You log into Roblox and everything is gone - your Robux, your limiteds, your whole inventory. Someone stole your account while you were sleeping.

It happens to thousands of players every day. Today you learn the exact moves that keep your account, your info, and your progress safe.

🔐 Pro Password Strategy

Hackers use scripts that guess thousands of passwords a second. Don't make it easy.

❌ NO NO's

Using your pet's name, your birthday, or "12345".

✅ PRO TIP

Use random words: PizzaDragonSkate88

Sentry Rule: Never share your password with friends online, even if they promise you free items.

🎣 The "Free Currency" Scam

⚠️ Watch Out For:

"Click here for 10,000 FREE ROBUX / V-BUCKS! Just log in to claim!"

  • The Trick: The link takes you to a fake login page to steal your account.
  • The Truth: No one gives away money for free online. If it's too good to be true, it's a scam.

🕵️ Spot the Scam: Real or Fake?

Quick round - you make the call:

  • 1. A DM says "Free 10,000 Robux, just log in on this site!" (Fake - it's a login stealer.)
  • 2. A "mod menu" download from a random Discord server promises free skins. (Fake - it's malware.)
  • 3. Your best friend from school texts you about homework. (Real - but a stranger claiming to be a kid your age? Different story.)

🕶️ Keep Your Real Life Private

Your profile is not your diary. Strangers can see more than you think.

❌ Never share:

Your real name, school, address, or where you hang out - not in your username, bio, or posts.

✅ Instead:

Use a made-up username, keep profiles private, and skip "what school do you go to?" questions.

Sentry Rule: Online friends know your game tag - not your home address.

👤 Strangers in Your DMs

On Discord and in games, anyone can pretend to be anyone. That "kid your age" might be an adult pretending.

  • Red flags: asks to move to a private chat, asks for photos, wants secrets from your parents, or offers gifts for "favors."
  • Your move: Don't answer personal questions, block, and tell an adult you trust.

🙋 Tell an Adult - Don't Handle It Alone

Something feels off? A weird DM, a threat, a scam that actually worked? That's not yours to carry by yourself.

What to do:

Screenshot it, block the account, and show a parent, teacher, or another adult you trust. You won't be in trouble - asking for help is the pro move.

🦠 The "Free Mod" Trap

Malware often hides inside "free hacks" or "game mods" found on YouTube or Discord.

The Risk:

Sketchy files can steal every password saved in your browser and use your computer to send spam.

Stay Safe:

Don't download files from strangers. Ask a parent before installing new software.

⌨️ Keyboard Watchers

Keyloggers are a gamer's worst nightmare. They watch every key you press.

The Danger:

Once installed, keyloggers log your game account credentials the exact moment you type them in.

Defense Tip:

Never run unknown `.exe` files from untrusted links or random Discord servers.

⭐ 3 Rules to Remember

1. Free = fake

Free Robux, free V-Bucks, free mods - they're bait for your account.

2. Private stays private

No real name, school, or location - and your password is yours alone.

3. Weird = tell someone

Anything sketchy goes straight to an adult. You're never in trouble for asking.
